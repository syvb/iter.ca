Here are the payment methods I prefer using. I might be able to use other methods if none of these work for you; please ask if this is the case. This page is for when I pay you - if you need to pay me please email me for instructions.

## International

- **Wise**: Send me your Wisetag or email.
- **Cryptocurrency**: Send me your Ethereum address and I can send you mainnet USDC. I can probably do other cryptocurrencies if you want. I don’t make any promise about the provenance of the coins I send you.
- **Charity**: I can donate the payment to a charity of your choice if it’s easy for me to do.

## Canada
- **e-Transfer**: let me know what email to use

## United States
- **Zelle**: let me know what email to use
- **Check**: let me know what name and mailing address to send it to

## Misc
- I might be able to do PayPal if you really need it, but I’d prefer not to use it
