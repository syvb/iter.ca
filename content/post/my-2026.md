2026 was a really interesting year for me. I quit my job at the end of last year, and did a lot of cool stuff this year while funemployed! This is going to be a year-in-review post, except the year isn’t quite over yet so I’ll include last December too.

## What I did
### 39c3
I was at the [39c3](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2025/infos/startpage.html) in Hamburg in December 2025. It was pretty fun, although it would have gone better if I spoke German. But still a pretty interesting experience seeing so many people from the German-ish hacker community.

### ARENA
I was in London in January (and a bit of February) for [ARENA](https://www.arena.education/), which is an AI safety upskilling thing, where you pair-program with someone as you go through the learnings. I liked it, and I think it was pretty helpful for learning about AI safety stuff. It’s getting a little outdated now that we’re in a world where coding is solved (e.g. getting good at wrangling PyTorch tensors into the right shape is a lot less useful now that Claude is superhuman at einops), but was still pretty helpful for me, especially with conceptual understanding. I think it would be helpful if they focused more on that in the future, although I’m not sure *how* they would do that.

### Montreal
I spent a month in Montreal. It was fine. Montreal has pretty good city-vibes - it’s nice to walk around in, the buildings look nice, it’s fun to explore. And the public transit network is amazing - the subway system is very smooth and fun to use.

Unfortunately, there’s not a whole lot of interesting stuff going on there (at least that was relevant to my interests). There’s a bit of a local rationalist community but there’s really just not that much going on in Montreal. Which is sad, because it’s such a great city! I really liked exploring it!

I did some AI safety research while I was here, and ended up writing an [okay post about Claude's behavior](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/mYM9EAAhpbYDDmA3e/what-secret-goals-does-claude-think-it-has) but overall I didn't get much done in the way of AI research while I was here. I wish I had got more done!

### Inkhaven
I spent April living at [Lighthaven](https://lighthaven.space/) for [Inkhaven](https://www.inkhaven.blog/), which was a thing where you had to write a blog post every day. I liked it a lot; it was very fun living at Lighthaven and doing a lot of writing. (See [my Inkhaven retrospective](https://iter.ca/post/ink2/) for more details.)

I had intended to do some AI safety research while I was here, and I did do a little bit but not as much as I was originally hoping. I did write some somewhat-okay blog posts about AI while I was here though.

### Toronto
I was back in Toronto for May, in between Inkhaven and festival season. Toronto was fine. It has worse city vibes than Montreal, but it has the advantage that there’s much more interesting stuff happening in it! I did some interpretability research and spent some time talking with other people about AI safety. I wasn't super productive but I did do some interesting research while I was here.

### Festival season
I was back to living at Lighthaven from June 5-15 for [LessOnline](https://less.online)+Manifest, and summer camp between those two. Festival season was amazing and great! I really liked talking with everyone! I also really liked staying at Lighthaven again; it's a really amazing place to be at!

### Bay Area
I decided to spend ~6 weeks after festival season hanging out in the SF bay area, and it was overall really good! I spent a while in SF and Berkeley, and it was really great going to so many events and meeting so many people. The bay area is pretty magical to me; it feels like the only place in the world that has enough people there that I really jibe with. I did some natural language autoencoder research and did an okay job of doing some AI safety research while I was here, although I could've done better.

### Toronto interlude
I spent two weeks between SF and London in Toronto, mostly doing some logistical stuff that had to be done while I was in my home country. I went to the Ontario rationality weekend when I got back and it was nice talking with many of the people I know from Canada. It was nice visiting several places in the Greater Toronto Area. I visited Oakville for a bit and the provincial park there was pretty nice! Aside from that the city of Oakville seems like a terrible place to live; I don't understand why anyone would want to live there.

### Eleven hours in Montreal
You thought I was done with Montreal this year? Wrong! It turns out it was ~$100 cheaper to add an 11 hour Montreal connection on my flight from Toronto to London. Visiting Montreal was already appealing, and Air Canada effectively *paying me* $100 to walk through some Montreal parks with a friend who was in Montreal was an offer that was hard to refuse.

### London
I ended up going to London next, and I've been here in London since August. It’s pretty good, although not as good as SF. I fear I'm not as productive on research here, probably in large part because there's a lot less chances to talk with other people with similar interests here. I think I should probably spend more time visiting LISA and other related places while I'm here.

### Looking forward
I’m probably going to return to Toronto in mid-December (possibly with a stop in Paris on the way, but not sure yet). I might go to 40c3; I have tickets but I’m not sure if my travel schedule will make it possible to go. I’m still working out what I’m going to do after that, but I think it’s going to be pretty exciting! :)

## Reflections
I had a really fun year overall - it was great doing so many things and getting to see so many things and meet a lot of people! I wish I had gone to London in previous years; it’s a really nice place and there are a lot of cool things to do here.

### Did I win?
I could have been much better at doing things! Like I feel like I was sooo unproductive! I could have been so much better if I had just focused on things and did stuff. I really need to get more productive!! I feel like Inkhaven, and to a lesser extent being in SF, helped a lot with my productivity. Having a goal that I really had to meet every day helped me focus on achieving it; maybe I should do that more (e.g. more stuff like [Writehaven](/post/writehaven)).

I feel like I was really unproductive this year relative to what I could’ve been at if I had just locked in. I just need to lock in. I so so need to lock in. **I need to lock in.**
